## Portable release runtime

The portable release contains an unmodified CPython runtime. Its complete notices are included as `PYTHON-LICENSE.txt` in the release. CPython's bundled extensions also use [OpenSSL](docs/licenses/OpenSSL.txt), [libffi](docs/licenses/libffi.txt) and [zlib](docs/licenses/zlib.txt); their license texts are preserved here and in release packages. Windows runtime DLLs retain their Microsoft copyright notices.

Packaging uses [PyInstaller](https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html), whose bootloader exception permits distribution of the resulting application under its own license. PyInstaller itself is a build dependency.
